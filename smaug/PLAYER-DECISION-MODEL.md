# Smaug Player Decision Model

## Purpose

This document connects Smaug's decision vocabulary to four fictional evaluation dimensions: **Use**, **User**, **Moral Concretion**, and **Vibrancy of Player**.

These terms are interpretive project vocabulary. They describe the context in which Smaug evaluates a game, property, decision, or interaction. They are not measurements of human worth, intelligence, morality, or legal status.

## Four dimensions

### Use

**Use** asks what practical purpose an action, property, or decision serves. Smaug may consider utility, relevance, reversibility, and whether the proposed use is consistent with the declared task.

### User

**User** records the human-facing context of an action: who is operating or affected by the workflow, what role the user has declared, and what evidence is available. User context is not authority by itself.

### Moral Concretion

**Moral Concretion** means turning a vague normative concern into explicit, reviewable constraints. Smaug should prefer concrete rules and evidence over hidden assumptions about a person's character.

### Vibrancy of Player

**Vibrancy of Player** describes the liveliness of the current game or interaction: activity, meaningful change, engagement, and the presence of new decisions or properties worth observing. It is not a psychological or medical assessment.

## Intelligent vocabulary view

Smaug may compare these dimensions, identify which vocabulary is most relevant to the current game, preserve uncertainty, and propose a bounded decision. The model must not convert a score into sovereign authority over a person.

The intended path is:

`Castle -> Begin -> Player/Game -> Observe -> interpret vocabulary -> Match -> Decide/Review -> action -> record result -> next Game`

## Scoring convention

The implementation uses a simple bounded `0..100` scale for each dimension. The scores are decision inputs, not measurements of a person.

The highest dimension may be recorded as the **primary vocabulary** for diagnostic explanation. The arithmetic average provides a basic confidence signal. A lower-confidence result remains in OBSERVE and can be routed to REVIEW rather than being treated as an automatic authorization.

## Relationship to Hit Dice

Important games, properties, and decisions can produce recorded outcomes. Within the fictional Smaug progression system, those outcomes may contribute to Hit Dice progression. Hit Dice represent fictional system capability/progression, not human combat ability or intelligence.

A Pearly Wisdom Roll may therefore sit inside the progression loop:

`Game -> Roll -> Result -> Hit Dice change -> stronger fictional capability -> next Game`

## Human control

Player vocabulary is descriptive and reviewable. It must not be used to punish, demean, profile, or deny a person services. Material consequences require explicit policy, evidence, and appropriate human review.
