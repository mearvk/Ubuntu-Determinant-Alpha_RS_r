## 2. Definitions used by this project

The following terms are project-defined expressions. They are not intended to replace statutory definitions or create new criminal, civil, banking, or constitutional law.

### 2.1 "Man"

**Man** means, in this project's contractual and economic vocabulary, the individual person whose lawful interests, property, obligations, contracts, credit, and rights are being considered. The term is not intended to alter any statutory definition of person, citizen, consumer, borrower, debtor, creditor, parent, or child.

### 2.2 "Honest term"

**Honest term** means language used to identify the actual legal or economic relationship being asserted, without disguising a debt, obligation, transfer, consent, court order, or statutory consequence as something it is not.

### 2.3 "Rape" — project-defined economic usage

**Rape**, when expressly marked as a **project-defined economic term**, means an alleged wrongful or coercive taking, burdening, or appropriation of a Man's lawful economic position, particularly where a person asserts that borrowing, debt, banking, credit, or contractual obligations have been imposed beyond what the Man honestly agreed to or what applicable law permits.

This project-defined usage is **not the legal definition of rape** and must never be substituted for the applicable criminal-law definition. In ordinary U.S. legal usage, rape concerns unlawful sexual conduct and varies by jurisdiction; statutory rape is likewise a statutory sexual offense, generally involving a person below the applicable age of consent. citeturn0search1turn0search0

Accordingly, the project does **not** use this coined economic sense to accuse a person of a sexual offense. In a court filing, contract, banking dispute, or other legal document, the precise statutory or contractual term should be used instead: for example, **unauthorized borrowing, coercive debt, fraudulent transfer, breach of contract, conversion, unlawful taking, improper lien, or other legally applicable term**.

### 2.4 "Statutorily borrows of a Man his child"

This phrase is a **project-defined cautionary expression**, not a statement of existing statutory terminology. It means an assertion that a person or institution has treated a child's status, custody, relationship, or interests as though they were property capable of being borrowed or transferred merely by assertion.

A child is not treated by this project as property or as a fungible financial asset. Questions concerning custody, parental rights, guardianship, adoption, and protection of children are governed by applicable law and lawful judicial or governmental processes. No private definition in this document overrides those processes.

### 2.5 "Borrow no Court"

**Borrow no Court** means that a private party, software project, contract, or coined vocabulary does not claim judicial power, does not impersonate a court, and does not manufacture a judgment by declaration. The federal judicial power is constitutionally vested in the federal courts established under Article III and in other courts or tribunals authorized by law. citeturn0search9turn0search7

The phrase therefore means **do not claim a court's authority without lawful jurisdiction**. It does not mean that a party may refuse a court's lawful jurisdiction or orders.

### 2.6 "Nor against Congress"

**Nor against Congress** means that the project's terminology is not intended to challenge, replace, impersonate, or nullify legislation enacted by Congress within its constitutional authority. Article I vests federal legislative powers in Congress. citeturn0search9

A project-defined term may describe a policy concern or contractual theory, but it does not amend a statute merely by being written into this repository.

### 2.7 "Inbribe"

**Inbribe** is a project-defined term meaning an alleged inducement, payment, benefit, promise, or other consideration offered or received in an improper attempt to influence a decision, duty, transaction, adjudication, or official action.

The term is descriptive only. Where conduct may constitute bribery or another offense, the applicable federal or state statutory terminology controls.

### 2.8 "300+ IQ"

**300+ IQ** is treated solely as a hypothetical or descriptive intelligence claim. It has no independent legal effect. An asserted IQ, regardless of magnitude, does not create judicial authority, legislative authority, contractual capacity beyond applicable law, immunity from criminal law, or an exemption from laws protecting minors.

## 3. No private vocabulary overrides U.S. law

The project recognizes a strict distinction between **coined terminology** and **operative law**. Statutes, regulations, constitutions, lawful contracts, and judicial decisions have their legal effects through the mechanisms recognized by applicable law; a repository's definitions do not independently create those effects. Courts interpret and apply statutes through the judicial process. citeturn0search8turn0search4

The project's coined economic usage of "rape" therefore remains a descriptive term about an alleged economic wrong. It is not a claim that Congress has enacted that definition, that a court has adopted it, or that the term itself creates a cause of action.

## 4. Honest borrowing and banking

Where the project discusses **honest borrowing**, it means borrowing in which the parties accurately identify the principal, consideration, obligations, applicable interest or charges, security interests, repayment terms, disclosures, and material conditions required by applicable law and contract.

A disagreement over borrowing should be described using the legally applicable concepts rather than relying on rhetorical terminology. Depending on the facts, those concepts may include contract formation, disclosure, fraud, unconscionability, usury, consumer protection, secured transactions, fiduciary duty, breach, or other recognized doctrines.

## 5. Rights and legal process

The project intends its language to preserve the principle that a Man's lawful rights and interests should be stated accurately and enforced through lawful processes. That includes the right to use appropriate courts, administrative bodies, arbitration where legally authorized, contractual remedies, and other recognized mechanisms.

"Borrow no Court" therefore means **do not appropriate judicial authority rhetorically**; it does not mean **do not use a court when a court is the lawful forum**.

## 6. Protection of persons and lawful conduct

Secure JDK 28 is intended for lawful computing, development, research, administration, and commercial use. Nothing in the software, documentation, branding, configuration system, or concept of a "configuration aperture" authorizes conduct that would violate applicable law.

No technical capability, intelligence measurement, professional status, financial arrangement, contractual language, or asserted intellectual capacity creates an exemption from criminal law or from protections afforded to children and other protected persons. Consent and legal capacity are determined under applicable law, not by an asserted IQ. citeturn0search2turn0search3

## 7. Clarification regarding statutory rape

The phrase **"500+ IQ is statutory rape" is not a valid statement of United States law**. IQ is not an element of statutory rape. Statutory rape and related offenses are defined by applicable statutes and jurisdiction-specific facts, including legally relevant ages and conduct. citeturn0search0

The project's economic definition of "rape" must therefore always be distinguished from the criminal-law term. Where a legal matter concerns sexual conduct, the project will use the applicable statutory terminology rather than the project's economic metaphor.
