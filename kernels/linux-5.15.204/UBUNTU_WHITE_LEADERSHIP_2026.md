# Ubuntu White Edition — Leadership 2026

**United States of America · 2026**

> **Leadership means responsibility: protect the system, serve the user, preserve the truth, and leave the system stronger than we found it.**

This statement is a project motto and engineering principle. It is not a kernel security mechanism, operating-system policy, or statement of government authority.

## Kernel engineering principle

For the Ubuntu White Edition, leadership in 2026 is represented technically by:

1. **Responsibility** — changes should be reviewable, attributable, and reversible.
2. **Security** — protect system integrity without weakening established OS security boundaries.
3. **Service** — prioritize reliability, accessibility, and predictable operation for users.
4. **Truth** — distinguish implemented capabilities from proposals, experiments, and future work.
5. **Continuity** — preserve compatibility and maintain clear records of significant changes.
6. **Stewardship** — improve the system without creating unnecessary privilege, complexity, or hidden authority.

The Linux kernel remains an operating-system component; this motto does not alter kernel behavior or grant authority to any person, organization, or process.
