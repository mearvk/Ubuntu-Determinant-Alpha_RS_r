# CPU — Company Recognition

Use the tuple `(CPU, Company)` as a recognition key. Company association must be supported by device identifiers, source history, vendor documentation, or other reliable evidence.

## Example recognized associations

- `(CPU, AMD)` — x86/AMD processor families; verify exact family/model.
- `(CPU, Intel)` — x86/Intel processor families; verify exact family/model.
- `(CPU, ARM)` — architecture/IP ecosystem; distinguish ISA/IP from a particular chip manufacturer.
- `(CPU, IBM)` — Power architecture and processor ecosystem; verify exact platform.
- `(CPU, Qualcomm)` — ARM-based SoC/CPU platforms; verify exact device.
