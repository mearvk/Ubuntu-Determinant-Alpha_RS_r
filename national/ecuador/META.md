# Ecuador Archive Metadata

## Scope

- Geography: Republic of Ecuador (national aggregate)
- Archive period: 1965-2026
- Archive class: historical economic, agricultural, food, livestock, produce, and water documentation
- Compiler: MEARVK LLC / Max Rupplin — archive compiler
- Intended audiences: college/academic research, professional analysis, government/public-institution research, congressional/public-policy work, legal/historical research, institutional archives, and general reference

## Source posture

The archive is source-aware and evidence-led. INEC (Instituto Nacional de Estadistica y Censos) is the preferred national primary family where a compatible machine-readable series exists. For deep historical coverage (1961 onward) and reproducibility, FAOSTAT, World Bank WDI, and FAO AQUASTAT provide the international baseline.

## Science Grade

6+ — documentation-quality grade for structured archival work. It is not a claim of experimental validity, causal identification, or statistical significance.

## Methods — WHY

The annual documents exist so later readers can distinguish primary observations, historical definitions, derived measures, and unresolved gaps. The archive preserves negative knowledge: unverified series are recorded as `N/A` rather than manufactured.

## Source discipline

1. Prefer original government/statistical publications where a compatible series exists.
2. Preserve the source year and publication/revision date.
3. Preserve units and definitions exactly before deriving alternate measures.
4. Mark missing, incompatible, reconstructed, or estimated observations explicitly.
5. Do not backfill historical years from modern summaries unless the summary documents the historical series.
6. Derived model outputs must remain distinguishable from source observations.

## Annual document rule

For each `YYYY/` directory, `YYYY/META.md` controls metadata and `YYYY/INDEX.md` indexes the ten domain documents. Every domain document refers back to `./META.md`.

See `../../countries/MASTER.md` for the archive-wide tracker and its Sources section.
