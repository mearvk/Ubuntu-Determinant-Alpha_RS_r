# China Oil-Bearing Crops

## 2025 production

China reported **40.96 million tonnes** of oil-bearing crop output in 2025, an increase of **2.9%** from 2024. Sown area was approximately **14.45 million hectares**.

## Main economic crops

Important oil-bearing crops include soybean, peanut, rapeseed and other oilseeds. They provide edible oils, protein meals, feed inputs, and industrial raw materials.

## Soybeans

2025 soybean output was **20.91 million tonnes**, up **1.3%**. Soybean economics are linked to domestic crushing, edible-oil production, livestock feed, protein meals, and international trade.

## Food-system role

Oil crops occupy an important position between crop agriculture and animal agriculture because crushing produces both edible oil and protein-rich meal. The latter is an important feed input.

## Source

National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2025 grain and national economic statistics.
