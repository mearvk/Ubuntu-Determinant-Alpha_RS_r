## Farming technology

NBS reported that agricultural science and technology contributed more than 64% of agricultural growth/progress measurement in 2025, improved-variety coverage exceeded 96%, and the comprehensive mechanization rate for crop ploughing, planting and harvesting reached 76.7%.

## Crop structure

The 2025 grain area included approximately 44.96 million ha of corn, 29.01 million ha of rice, 23.58 million ha of wheat, and 10.26 million ha of soybeans. Oil-bearing crops occupied 14.45 million ha, while cotton occupied 2.98 million ha.

## Five-Year-Plan comparison

Between 2020 and 2025, NBS reported growth in economic-crop area of 7.4%, vegetables including edible fungi of 18.6%, orchard fruit and melons of 23.4%, oil-bearing crop output of 14.2%, tea of 33.6%, and cotton of 12.4%.
