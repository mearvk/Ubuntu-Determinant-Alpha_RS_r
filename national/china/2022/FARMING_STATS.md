# China 2022 — Farming Statistics

Source: National Bureau of Statistics, 2022 Statistical Communiqué.

- Grain sown area: 118.33 million ha.
- Rice: 29.45m ha; wheat: 23.52m ha; corn: 43.07m ha; soybean: 10.24m ha.
- Farmland newly equipped with irrigation: 0.78 million ha.
- Farmland newly equipped with water-saving irrigation: 1.61 million ha.
