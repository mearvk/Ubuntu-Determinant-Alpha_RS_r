# China — 2006 Water

- New effective irrigation area: **108 ten-thousand ha** (1.08 million ha).
- New water-saving irrigation area: **128 ten-thousand ha** (1.28 million ha).
- Total water resources: **25,500 hundred million m³**.
- Total water use: **5,670 hundred million m³**.
- Agricultural water use growth: **0.2%**.
- Aquatic products: **5,250 ten-thousand tonnes**.

Source: NBS 2006 Statistical Communiqué.
