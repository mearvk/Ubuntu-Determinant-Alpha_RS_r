# China Cotton Production

## 2025 baseline

China's cotton sown area was approximately **2.98 million hectares** in 2025. Output reached **6.64 million tonnes**, up **7.7%** from 2024 and **12.4% above 2020** according to NBS's Five-Year-Plan comparison.

## Economic chain

Cotton connects farming with ginning, spinning, textiles, apparel, technical textiles, logistics, and international trade. Weather, irrigation, input costs, mechanization, quality, and textile demand affect the economics of the crop.

## Production geography

Xinjiang is a major cotton-producing region, while other areas contribute smaller quantities. Irrigation and mechanized production are particularly important in major production zones.

## Source

National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2025 national statistics and 2026 agricultural achievement report.
