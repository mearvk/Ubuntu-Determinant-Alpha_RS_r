# China 2018 — Farming Statistics

- Grain sown area: 117.04 million ha.
- Wheat: 24.27 million ha; rice: 30.19 million ha; corn: 42.13 million ha.
- Cotton: 3.35 million ha; oil-bearing crops: 12.89 million ha; sugar crops: 1.63 million ha.
- New effective irrigation: over 0.72 million ha.
- New water-saving irrigation: another 1.44 million ha.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2018 Statistical Communiqué.
