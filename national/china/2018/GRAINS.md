# China 2018 — Grains

- Total grain: 657.89 million tons.
- Summer grain: 138.78 million tons.
- Early rice: 28.59 million tons.
- Autumn grain: 490.52 million tons.
- Rice: 212.13 million tons; wheat: 131.43 million tons; corn: 257.33 million tons.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2018 Statistical Communiqué.
