# China 2018 — Economy

- GDP: 90,030.9 billion yuan; growth 6.6%.
- Primary industry: 6,473.4 billion yuan; growth 3.5%.
- Secondary industry: 36,600.1 billion yuan; growth 5.8%.
- Tertiary industry: 46,957.5 billion yuan; growth 7.6%.

Note: contemporaneous communiqué figures are preliminary.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2018 Statistical Communiqué.
