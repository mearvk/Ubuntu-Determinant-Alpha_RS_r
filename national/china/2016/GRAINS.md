# China 2016 — Grains

- Total grain: 616.24 million tons.
- Summer grain: 139.20 million tons.
- Early rice: 32.78 million tons.
- Autumn grain: 444.26 million tons.
- Rice: 206.93 million tons; wheat: 128.85 million tons; corn: 219.55 million tons.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2016 Statistical Communiqué.
