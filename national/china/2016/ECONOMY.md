# China 2016 — Economy

- GDP: 74,412.7 billion yuan; growth 6.7%.
- Primary industry: 6,367.1 billion yuan; growth 3.3%.
- Secondary industry: 29,623.6 billion yuan; growth 6.1%.
- Tertiary industry: 38,422.1 billion yuan; growth 7.8%.

Note: contemporaneous communiqué figures are preliminary.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2016 Statistical Communiqué.
