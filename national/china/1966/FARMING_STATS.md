## Source notes

The National Bureau of Statistics describes its agricultural statistics as covering cultivated land, agricultural production, major agricultural products, agricultural machinery, and related rural statistics. Historical agricultural series are retained as reported observations rather than reconstructed estimates.

Primary reference: National Bureau of Statistics of China, Statistical Yearbooks: https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/Statisticaldata/yearbook/
