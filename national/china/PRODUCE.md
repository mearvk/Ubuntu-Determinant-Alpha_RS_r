# China Agricultural Produce

## Major produce groups

China's agricultural produce system includes:

- Grains and cereals: rice, wheat, corn and other grains.
- Beans and oilseeds: soybeans, peanuts, rapeseed and related crops.
- Tubers and root crops.
- Vegetables and edible fungi.
- Orchard fruit and melons.
- Tea.
- Cotton and other fiber crops.
- Sugar crops.
- Pork, beef, mutton and poultry.
- Milk and eggs.
- Farmed and captured aquatic products.
- Timber and other forest products.

## 2025 scale markers

Official NBS statistics provide several useful physical-production anchors:

- Grain: **714.88 Mt**.
- Vegetables including edible fungi: **about 890 Mt**.
- Orchard fruit and melons: **about 350 Mt**.
- Pork, beef, mutton and poultry: **100.72 Mt**.
- Aquatic products: **76.57 Mt**.
- Milk: **40.91 Mt**.
- Eggs: **34.98 Mt**.
- Oil-bearing crops: **40.96 Mt**.
- Sugar crops: **117.01 Mt**.
- Tea: **3.92 Mt**.
- Cotton: **6.64 Mt**.

## Why these measures matter

Physical output describes the scale of the production system but is not equivalent to economic value. Prices, processing, imports and exports, transportation, inventories, labor, land, capital, and consumer demand determine economic value.

## Source

National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2025 national economic and social development statistics and 2026 agricultural achievement reports.
