# China Dairy and Egg Production

## 2025 output

- Milk: **40.91 million tonnes**, up **0.3%** year over year.
- Eggs: **34.98 million tonnes**, down **2.5%** year over year.

## Economic system

Dairy production connects cattle breeding, feed, forage, veterinary services, milk collection, refrigeration, processing, packaging, distribution, and retail. Egg production connects layer poultry, feed, hatcheries, veterinary services, grading, processing, logistics, and retail.

## Feed connection

Milk and egg production depend on stable feed supplies. Grain, oilseed meal, forage, water, energy, and veterinary inputs therefore link these sectors to the wider agricultural economy.

## Source

National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2025 national economic and social development statistics.
