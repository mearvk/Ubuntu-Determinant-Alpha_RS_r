## Settlement Class
Descriptive, source-dependent social/economic metadata, not a legal determination. Controlled vocabulary: Democrat, Millionaire, Professional, College/Academic, Government/Public Institution, Worker, Farmer, Business/Commercial, Legal, Congressional/Public Policy, Institutional, Other, or `UNCLASSIFIED`. Never infer a class without support; labels are not moral or legal conclusions.

## Methods Intent — WHY
Preserve historical observations with provenance for college/professional study, long-term legal and historical research, and Congressional, governmental, and institutional inquiry. The moral purpose is to prevent convenient rewriting: original observation, reconstruction, official statistic, secondary estimate, and interpretation must remain distinguishable.
