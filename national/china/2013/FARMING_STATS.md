# China 2013 — Farming Statistics

- Grain sown area: 111.95 million ha.
- Cotton sown area: 4.35 million ha.
- Oil-bearing crops sown area: 14.08 million ha.
- Sugar crops sown area: 1.99 million ha.
- New effective irrigation area: over 1.29 million ha.
- New water-saving irrigation area: over 2.11 million ha.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2013 Statistical Communiqué.
