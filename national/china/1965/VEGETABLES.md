The parent directory identifies the year. This document covers vegetable production as part of China's horticultural and food-supply system.

## Methods

The purpose of this record is to preserve what can actually be established about vegetables in the historical food system, including the important distinction between a production observation and a commodity-price observation. It is intended for **college study, professional agricultural and economic analysis, and long-term research**. The WHY is to make absence of evidence visible rather than silently replacing it with a later price, an estimate, or an unrelated index.

The record may also support **legal, congressional, institutional, and moral inquiry** when food supply, public policy, resource allocation, or historical conditions are evaluated. Its method is deliberately conservative: document the evidence, preserve the definition, identify uncertainty, and allow later researchers to improve the record without erasing its provenance. This is not legal advice or political advocacy; it is a framework for accountable historical evidence.

## Data rule
The archive does not substitute a later-period vegetable price, a price index, or an estimated value for a 1965 national commodity quotation. Vegetable production and price observations remain separate until a period-specific source is verified.
