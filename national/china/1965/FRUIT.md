# Fruit — Annual Statistical Record

The parent directory identifies the year. Fruit covers orchard, tree-fruit and related horticultural production included in official agricultural statistics.

> **Metadata:** `record_type=annual_fruit` · `year=1965` · `geography=China` · `scope=national` · `status=historical_actual` · `units=source_specific` · `price_basis=commodity_price` · `methodology=historical_source_preservation`

## Methods

The purpose of this record is to preserve historical fruit evidence in a form that remains useful for **college and university study, professional agricultural and economic work, and long-term research**. The WHY is to keep production, pricing, definitions, and uncertainty separate enough that a later reader can understand the historical food system rather than inherit an unexplained number.

Fruit statistics can also contribute to **legal, congressional, institutional, and moral inquiry** concerning food systems, public policy, resource allocation, and historical conditions. The archive therefore refuses to manufacture a price where a period-specific national observation has not been verified. Its purpose is accountable evidence: preserve what is known, identify what is not, and leave a defensible trail for future researchers. It is not legal advice or political advocacy.
