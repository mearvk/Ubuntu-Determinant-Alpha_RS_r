# China 2023 — Agriculture

- Grain sown area: 118.97 million ha.
- Rice: 28.95m ha; wheat: 23.63m; corn: 44.22m; soybean: 10.47m.
- Cotton: 2.79m ha; oil-bearing: 13.92m; sugar crops: 1.41m ha.
- Grain output: 695.41 million tons.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics, 2023 Statistical Communiqué.
