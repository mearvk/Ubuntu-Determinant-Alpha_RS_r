# China 2014 — Agriculture

- Grain sown area: 112.74 million ha.
- Cotton sown area: 4.22 million ha.
- Oil-bearing crops sown area: 14.08 million ha.
- Sugar crops sown area: 1.91 million ha.
- Grain output: 607.10 million tons.
- Cotton: 6.16 million tons.
- Oil-bearing crops: 35.17 million tons.
- Sugar crops: 134.03 million tons.
- Tea: 2.09 million tons.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2014 Statistical Communiqué.
