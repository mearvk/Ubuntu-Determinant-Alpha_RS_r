# China 2014 — Water

- Water resources: 2,837.0 billion m³.
- Average precipitation: 648 mm.
- Large reservoirs monitored: 609; year-end storage 366.3 billion m³.
- Total water consumption: 622.0 billion m³.
- Water consumption per 10,000 yuan GDP: 112 m³.
- Per-capita water consumption: 456 m³.
- Aquatic products: 64.50 million tons; cultured 47.62 million; fished 16.88 million.
- New effective irrigation: over 1.32 million ha.
- New water-saving irrigation: over 2.23 million ha.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2014 Statistical Communiqué; water-resource data attributed there to the Ministry of Water Resources.
