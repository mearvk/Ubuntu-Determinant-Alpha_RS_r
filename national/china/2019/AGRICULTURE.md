# China 2019 — Agriculture

## Status
AVAILABLE

## Key observations
- Grain sown area: **116.06 million hectares**.
- Wheat: **23.73 million ha**; rice: **29.69 million ha**; corn: **41.28 million ha**.
- Cotton: **3.34 million ha**; oil-bearing crops: **12.93 million ha**; sugar crops: **1.62 million ha**.
- Grain output: **663.84 million tons**.

## Source
National Bureau of Statistics, 2019 Statistical Communiqué.
