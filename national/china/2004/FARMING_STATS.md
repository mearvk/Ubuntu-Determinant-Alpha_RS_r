# China — 2004 Farming Statistics

## Verified annual field statistics
- Grain sown area: **101.61 million ha**.
- Cotton sown area: **5.69 million ha**.
- Oil-bearing crop sown area: **14.52 million ha**.
- Sugar-crop sown area: **1.57 million ha**.
- Vegetable sown area: **17.67 million ha**.
- Grain production: **469.47 million t**.
- Oil-bearing crops: **30.57 million t**.

## Source
National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2004 Statistical Communiqué: https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/NewsEvents/200502/t20050228_25600.html
