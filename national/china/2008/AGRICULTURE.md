# China — 2008 Agriculture

- Grain sown area: 106.70 million ha
- Cotton sown area: 5.76 million ha
- Oil-bearing crop sown area: 12.71 million ha
- Sugar-crop sown area: 1.93 million ha
- Grain output: 52,850万吨
- Cotton: 750万吨 (later NBS revised: 749万吨)
- Oil-bearing crops: 2,950万吨 (later revised: 2,953万吨)
- Sugar crops: 13,000万吨 (later revised: 13,420万吨)

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China.
