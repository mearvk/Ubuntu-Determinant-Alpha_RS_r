# China — 2008 Produce

- Cotton: 750万吨; later revised: 749万吨
- Oil-bearing crops: 2,950万吨; later revised: 2,953万吨
- Sugar crops: 13,000万吨; later revised: 13,420万吨
- Tobacco: 260万吨
- Tea: 124万吨
- Vegetables + edible fungi: 59,240万吨 (later NBS revised series)
- Fruit: 11,339万吨 (garden fruit; excludes fruit-use melons; later revised series)

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China.
