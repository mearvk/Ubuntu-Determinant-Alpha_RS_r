# China 2017 — Economy

- GDP: 82,712.2 billion yuan; growth 6.9%.
- Primary industry: 6,546.8 billion yuan; growth 3.9%.
- Secondary industry: 33,462.3 billion yuan; growth 6.1%.
- Tertiary industry: 42,703.2 billion yuan; growth 8.0%.

Note: contemporaneous communiqué figures are preliminary.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2017 Statistical Communiqué.
