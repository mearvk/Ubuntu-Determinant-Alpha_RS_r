# China 2017 — Agriculture

- Grain sown area: 112.22 million ha.
- Wheat: 23.99 million ha; rice: 30.18 million ha; corn: 35.45 million ha.
- Cotton: 3.23 million ha.
- Oil-bearing crops: 14.20 million ha.
- Sugar crops: 1.68 million ha.
- Grain output: 617.91 million tons.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2017 Statistical Communiqué.
