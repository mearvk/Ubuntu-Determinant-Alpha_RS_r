# China 2015 — Water

- Water resources: 2,830.6 billion m³.
- Average precipitation: 644 mm.
- Large reservoirs monitored: 614; year-end storage 364.5 billion m³.
- Total water consumption: 618.0 billion m³.
- Water consumption per 10,000 yuan GDP: 104 m³.
- Per-capita water consumption: 450 m³.
- Aquatic products: 66.90 million tons; cultured 49.42 million; fished 17.48 million.
- New effective irrigation: over 1.58 million ha.
- New water-saving irrigation: over 2.54 million ha.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2015 Statistical Communiqué; water-resource data attributed there to the Ministry of Water Resources.
