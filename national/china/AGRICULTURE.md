# China Agriculture

## 2025 production baseline

China's agricultural system is dominated by intensive crop production, livestock, aquaculture, horticulture, forestry, and increasingly mechanized farming.

- Grain sown area: **119.41 million hectares**.
- Grain output: **714.88 million tonnes**.
- Grain yield: about **5,987 kg/ha**.
- Grain output increased **1.2%** from 2024.
- Economic-crop sown area reached about **820 million mu** (about 54.7 million hectares), according to NBS's Five-Year-Plan review.
- Agricultural science and technology contribution was reported at **more than 64%** in 2025.
- Improved-variety coverage was reported above **96%**.
- Comprehensive mechanization rate for crop ploughing, planting and harvesting reached **76.7%**.

## Major agricultural branches

1. Grain: rice, wheat, corn, soybeans and other cereals, beans and tubers.
2. Oil crops: soybean, peanut, rapeseed and other oil-bearing crops.
3. Fiber crops: especially cotton.
4. Sugar crops: principally sugarcane and sugar beet.
5. Vegetables and edible fungi.
6. Fruits and melons.
7. Tea.
8. Livestock: pigs, cattle, sheep and poultry.
9. Dairy and egg production.
10. Freshwater and marine aquaculture and capture fisheries.
11. Forestry and timber.

## Structural characteristics

Agriculture is geographically differentiated. Rice is particularly important in wetter southern and central regions; wheat and corn occupy major areas in northern and northeastern production zones; livestock is distributed across intensive commercial systems and extensive pastoral regions; fruit and vegetable production is spread across many climate zones.

## Agriculture and the wider economy

Agriculture is more than the farm-gate primary sector. China's official agriculture-and-related-industries accounting includes processing, manufacturing, logistics, circulation, and other connected activities. In 2024 this wider system represented 15.29% of GDP.
