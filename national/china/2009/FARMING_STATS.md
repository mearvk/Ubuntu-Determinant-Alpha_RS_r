# China — 2009 Farming Statistics

- Grain sown area: 108.97 million ha
- Cotton sown area: 4.95 million ha
- Oil-bearing crop sown area: 13.60 million ha
- Sugar-crop sown area: 1.88 million ha
- New effective irrigation area: 1.471 million ha
- New water-saving irrigation area: 1.826 million ha

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China.
