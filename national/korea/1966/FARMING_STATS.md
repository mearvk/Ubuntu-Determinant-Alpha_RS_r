## Methods — WHY

This record separates observed statistics from unavailable observations. Historical survey definitions can change, so values are not back-cast merely to fill a table. Comparable observations may be added from KOSIS, MAFRA/KASS, FAOSTAT and World Bank series with the source definition preserved.

## Sources

- KOSIS national statistical database: Agriculture and Forestry subject tables.
- KOSIS Grain Consumption Survey.
- MAFRA/KASS agricultural and rural statistical publications.
- FAOSTAT and World Bank indicators are secondary cross-checks where definitions permit.
