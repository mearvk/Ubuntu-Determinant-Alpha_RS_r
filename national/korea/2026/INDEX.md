## Archive status

Year: `2026`

This index is the navigation record for the Korea annual statistical archive. Each domain document is maintained separately so that units, definitions, source years, and historical breaks can be documented without silently combining unlike series.

`N/A` means that a comparable observation has not been verified for the year; it does not mean zero. The 2026 archive year must not be interpreted as a completed calendar-year statistical record where source data are still partial.

## Methods — WHY

The index provides a stable entry point for academic, professional, institutional, historical, and analytical use. Domain-level separation preserves provenance and makes later source transcription auditable.
