These values are from NIHHS Vegetable Basic Statistics. Onion and garlic are left N/A in this production table because the cited excerpt used for this archive did not expose compatible production values for those two crops.

## Method
Production, cultivated area, production value, and consumption are separate measures. Missing observations are not inferred. `N/A` does not mean zero.
