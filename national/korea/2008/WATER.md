This record covers water resources relevant to South Korea, with emphasis on agricultural water, freshwater availability, irrigation context, precipitation, and water-use indicators where compatible annual observations are available.

## Method and evidence status
`SOURCE_STRUCTURED`: the archive schema is fixed and observations are not estimated when a compatible historical observation has not been verified. `N/A` means no value is asserted here, not zero water use or zero precipitation.

Water supply, withdrawal, consumption, irrigation area, precipitation, and renewable-water-resource estimates are separate statistical concepts. They must not be substituted for one another. Historical observations are retained only when their definition, unit, geography, and reference period are sufficiently compatible.

## Historical comparability
Korean water statistics span multiple agencies and survey systems. Definitions and coverage can change over time, particularly for irrigation infrastructure, agricultural withdrawals, and national water-resource accounting. The archive therefore avoids silent back-casting and preserves `N/A` where a compatible observation cannot be established.
