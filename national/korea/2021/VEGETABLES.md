| Onion production | thousand t | N/A |
| Garlic production | thousand t | N/A |

NIHHS Vegetable Basic Statistics supplies the verified total, cabbage, radish and chili figures above. Onion and garlic remain N/A because a compatible production observation was not exposed in the cited production table used for this archive.

## Method
Production, cultivated area, production value, and consumption are separate measures. Missing observations are not inferred. `N/A` does not mean zero.
