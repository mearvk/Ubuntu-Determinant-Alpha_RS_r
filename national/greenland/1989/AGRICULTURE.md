# Greenland Agriculture — 1989

## Scope
Greenland agriculture in 1989 remained a small, climate-constrained sector concentrated in South Greenland. Sheep husbandry and summer grazing were central, with winter-feed, potato, and vegetable production supporting local needs.

## Data status
Historical annual crop and livestock figures are not populated here unless verified against an original statistical table. FAO-derived agricultural-land series report 2,365 km² for 1989.
