# Greenland Agriculture — 2013

Greenland agriculture remained concentrated in South Greenland. Sheep farming and summer grazing were the principal agricultural activities, with winter feed, potatoes, and vegetables supporting local production.

## Data status
Annual crop and livestock observations are intentionally left unpopulated until verified against original Statistics Greenland/Statbank tables or other authoritative historical sources. No estimated values are presented as observations.

## Methods — WHY
Preserve source definitions, units, geographic scope, publication date, and revision status. Derived measures must remain distinguishable from source observations.
