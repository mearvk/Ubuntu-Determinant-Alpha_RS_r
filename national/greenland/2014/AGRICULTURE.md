# Greenland Agriculture — 2014

Greenland agriculture remained concentrated in South Greenland. Sheep farming and summer grazing were the principal agricultural activities, with winter feed, potatoes, and vegetables supporting local production.

## Verified reference data
Statistics Greenland reports 18,738 sheep, 3,000 tame reindeer, 143 cows, 123 horses, 174 fowls, and 4 beehives for 2014 in its agriculture series.

## Methods — WHY
The livestock figures above are retained as published reference observations. Crop series and additional agricultural measures require table-level verification before inclusion.
