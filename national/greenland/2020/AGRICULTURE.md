# Greenland Agriculture — 2020

Greenland agriculture remained concentrated in South Greenland, with sheep farming and summer grazing central to the sector. Winter feed, potatoes, and vegetables supported local production.

## Verified reference data
Statistics Greenland reports 18,105 sheep, 3,000 tame reindeer, 327 cows, 139 horses, 234 fowls, and no reported beehive count in its published agriculture series for 2020.

## Methods — WHY
The published livestock figures are retained as reference observations. Crop series and additional agricultural measures require table-level verification before inclusion.
