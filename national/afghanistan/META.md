# Afghanistan Archive Metadata

## Scope

- Geography: Afghanistan (national aggregate)
- Archive period: 1965-2026
- Archive class: historical economic, agricultural, food, livestock, produce, and water documentation
- Compiler: MEARVK LLC / Max Rupplin — archive compiler
- Intended audiences: college/academic research, professional analysis, government/public-institution research, congressional/public-policy work, legal/historical research, institutional archives, and general reference

## Source posture

The archive is source-aware and evidence-led. Afghan primary statistical institutions are preferred where a compatible machine-readable series exists. The National Statistics and Information Authority (NSIA) publishes national statistical yearbooks; the Ministry of Agriculture, Irrigation and Livestock (MAIL) is the principal source of crop estimates. Reliable Afghan crop series are strongest for recent years (broadly post-2008). For deeper historical coverage (1961 onward) and reproducibility, FAOSTAT, World Bank WDI, and FAO AQUASTAT provide the international baseline.

## Methods — WHY

The annual documents exist so that later readers can distinguish primary observations, historical definitions, derived measures, and unresolved gaps. This supports reproducible academic work, professional review, institutional continuity, and long-term legal or public-policy reference without silently converting assumptions into facts.

The archive also preserves negative knowledge: when a requested historical series has not yet been verified, the document records that status instead of manufacturing continuity. Afghanistan's statistical system underwent repeated disruption over the past four decades, so gaps are expected and are recorded as `N/A` rather than estimated.

## Source discipline

1. Prefer original government/statistical publications (NSIA, MAIL) where a compatible series exists.
2. Preserve the source year and publication/revision date.
3. Preserve units and definitions exactly before deriving alternate measures.
4. Mark missing, incompatible, reconstructed, or estimated observations explicitly.
5. Do not backfill historical years from modern summaries unless the summary explicitly documents the historical series.
6. Derived model outputs must remain distinguishable from source observations.

## Annual document rule

For each `YYYY/` directory, `YYYY/META.md` controls metadata and `YYYY/INDEX.md` indexes the ten domain documents. Every domain document must refer back to `./META.md` and identify its source status.

See `../../countries/MASTER.md` for the archive-wide country tracker and its Sources section.
