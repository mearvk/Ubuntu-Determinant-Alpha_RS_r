# China Economy

## Scope

This document provides a compact economic profile for the China module, with emphasis on production, agriculture, food systems, natural resources, and related economic activity. Statistics are dated so that later revisions can be distinguished from the baseline.

## 2025 macroeconomic baseline

According to the National Bureau of Statistics of China (NBS), preliminary 2025 GDP was **140,187.9 billion yuan**, an increase of **5.0%** at constant prices.

| Sector | 2025 value added (billion yuan) | Growth |
|---|---:|---:|
| Primary industry | 9,334.7 | 3.9% |
| Secondary industry | 49,965.3 | 4.5% |
| Tertiary industry | 80,887.9 | 5.4% |
| Agriculture, forestry, animal husbandry and fishery | 9,910.7 | 4.1% |
| Industry | 41,682.6 | 5.8% |
| Manufacturing | 34,674.7 | 6.1% |

## Agriculture's wider economic footprint

NBS reported that China's agriculture and related industries generated **20.6173 trillion yuan** of value added in 2024, equivalent to **15.29% of GDP**. This wider measure includes primary production, processing/manufacturing, and circulation services.

## Production system

China's economic production base is unusually broad. The food and agricultural system links crop farming, livestock, aquaculture, forestry, processing, cold-chain logistics, wholesale markets, retail, and exports/imports. Manufacturing, transport, energy, construction, finance, and services provide the infrastructure around that production system.
