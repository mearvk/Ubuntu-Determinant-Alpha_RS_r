# China Fruit Production

## 2025 baseline

NBS reported approximately **350 million tonnes** of orchard fruit and melons in 2025, an increase of **23.4% from 2020**.

## Major categories

China's fruit economy includes apples, citrus, pears, grapes, peaches, plums, bananas, kiwifruit, berries, tropical fruits, melons, and many regional specialty products.

## Production characteristics

Fruit production is strongly climate- and geography-dependent. Temperate fruit belts, subtropical production areas, and tropical southern regions support different crop mixes. Irrigation, orchard management, storage, grading, packaging, refrigerated logistics, and processing all influence realized economic value.

## Economic role

Fresh fruit supports farm income and domestic food consumption while processed fruit, juice, dried fruit, preserves, and other products extend the supply chain. Large urban markets create substantial demand for year-round distribution.

## Source and definition

The cited 350-million-tonne figure is the NBS aggregate for orchard fruit and melons in its 2025 Five-Year-Plan agricultural review. Product-level totals can differ between statistical systems because category definitions vary.
