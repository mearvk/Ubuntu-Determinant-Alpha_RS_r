# China Water Resources

## Role in the economy

Water is a foundational input to agriculture, households, industry, energy, cities, ecosystems, and inland fisheries. China's water geography is highly uneven: the south generally has greater water availability, while the north has greater pressure from demand and comparatively lower natural water availability.

## Major natural sources

- Major rivers: Yangtze, Yellow, Pearl, Huai, Hai, Liao, Songhua, and other river systems.
- Lakes and reservoirs.
- Groundwater and aquifers.
- Mountain snow and ice contribute to river systems in western and high-altitude regions.
- Precipitation is the primary replenishing mechanism for most surface and groundwater systems.

## 2022 official water-use reference

A Ministry of Water Resources water-resources bulletin reports national water use of approximately **581.29 billion m³** in 2022. Agriculture accounted for approximately **361.24 billion m³**, industry approximately **103.04 billion m³**, domestic use approximately **86.31 billion m³**, and artificial ecological/environmental replenishment approximately **30.70 billion m³**.

The same bulletin reports total water supply of about **581.29 billion m³**, including surface water, groundwater, and other sources. The figures are presented here as a dated historical reference rather than as a 2025 estimate.

## Regional structure

The Ministry's 2022 table divided the country into northern and southern major water-resource regions. It reported approximately 268.13 billion m³ of water supply in the six northern regions and 313.16 billion m³ in the four southern regions.

## Water and farming

Irrigation is critical to stable crop yields, particularly where precipitation is seasonal or insufficient. Water-saving irrigation, groundwater management, reservoirs, inter-basin transfers, drought planning, soil moisture management, and crop selection therefore have direct economic consequences.

## Data caution

Water accounting is highly dependent on year, hydrological conditions, statistical boundaries, and definitions of supply and use. Do not mix the 2022 bulletin figures with later-year totals without preserving the year and source.
