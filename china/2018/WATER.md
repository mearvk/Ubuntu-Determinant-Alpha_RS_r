# China 2018 — Water

- Water resources: 2,796.0 billion m³.
- Total water consumption: 611.0 billion m³.
- Water use per 10,000 yuan GDP: 73 m³.
- Per-capita water use: 439 m³.
- Aquatic products: 64.69 million tons; cultured 50.18 million; fished 14.51 million.
- New effective irrigation: over 0.72 million ha.
- New water-saving irrigation: 1.44 million ha.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2018 Statistical Communiqué; water data attributed to the Ministry of Water Resources.
