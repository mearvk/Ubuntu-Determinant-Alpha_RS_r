# China — 2007 Agriculture

- Grain sown area: 105.53 million ha
- Cotton sown area: 5.59 million ha
- Oil-bearing crop sown area: 10.94 million ha
- Sugar-crop sown area: 1.67 million ha
- Grain output: 50,150万吨
- Cotton: 760万吨 (later NBS revised series: 762万吨)
- Oil-bearing crops: 2,461万吨 (later revised: 2,569万吨)
- Sugar crops: 11,110万吨 (later revised: 12,188万吨)

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China. Later revised figures are explicitly identified and are not silently substituted for contemporaneous communiqué values.
