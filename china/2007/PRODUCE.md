# China — 2007 Produce

- Cotton: 760万吨; later NBS revised series: 762万吨
- Oil-bearing crops: 2,461万吨; later revised: 2,569万吨
- Sugar crops: 11,110万吨; later revised: 12,188万吨
- Tobacco: 239万吨
- Tea: 114万吨
- Vegetables + edible fungi: 56,452万吨 (later NBS revised series)
- Fruit: 10,520万吨 (garden fruit; excludes fruit-use melons; later NBS revised series)

The 2007 contemporaneous communiqué noted that fruit and vegetable output was still under verification; the later NBS revised series is therefore labeled rather than mixed into the preliminary series.
