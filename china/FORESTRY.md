# China Forestry

## 2025 timber output

NBS reported **139.37 million cubic metres** of timber production in 2025, down **1.1%** from 2024.

## Economic connections

Forestry links land management, timber production, engineered wood, furniture, construction materials, pulp and paper, biomass utilization, ecological services, and rural income.

## Resource-management considerations

Forestry statistics describe production, not the entire forest resource. Forest area, standing volume, plantation age structure, harvesting rules, restoration, biodiversity, carbon storage, and ecological-protection policies are separate dimensions and should not be conflated with annual timber output.

## Source

National Bureau of Statistics of China, *Statistical Communiqué of the PRC on the 2025 National Economic and Social Development*.
