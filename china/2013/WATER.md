# China 2013 — Water

- Water resources: 2,786.0 billion m³.
- Average precipitation: 665 mm.
- Large reservoirs monitored: 613; storage at year-end 348.8 billion m³.
- Total water consumption: 617.0 billion m³.
- Water consumption per 10,000 yuan GDP: 121 m³.
- Per-capita water consumption: 453 m³.
- Aquatic products: 61.72 million tons; cultured 45.47 million; fished 16.25 million.
- New effective irrigation: over 1.29 million ha.
- New water-saving irrigation: over 2.11 million ha.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2013 Statistical Communiqué; water-resource data attributed there to the Ministry of Water Resources.
