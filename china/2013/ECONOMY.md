# China 2013 — Economy

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, *Statistical Communiqué of the People's Republic of China on the 2013 National Economic and Social Development*.

- GDP: 56,884.5 billion yuan; growth 7.7%.
- Primary industry: 5,695.7 billion yuan; growth 4.0%.
- Secondary industry: 24,968.4 billion yuan; growth 7.8%.
- Tertiary industry: 26,220.4 billion yuan; growth 8.3%.
- GDP shares: primary 10.0%; secondary 43.9%; tertiary 46.1%.

Note: contemporaneous communiqué figures are preliminary.
