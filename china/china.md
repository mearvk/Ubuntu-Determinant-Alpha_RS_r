# China Import Evaluation

**Max Rupplin - MEARVK LLC 2026.**

## Purpose

This module evaluates China's import economy and associated supply-line conditions.
Observed trade data and modeled assumptions are deliberately separated.

## Current observations

| Measure | Value | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| 2025 total goods imports | ≈ $2.64T | Observed/official customs reporting |
| U.S. goods imports from China, 2025 | ≈ $308.7B | U.S. trade reporting |
| July 2026 import growth | 27.5% YoY | Reported customs data |

## Procedure

The model combines normalized import growth, high-technology exposure, and a
supply-line score. The weights are **45% / 30% / 25%** respectively.

`INT = 50` is only a modeled ordinal sensitivity value. It does **not** represent
IQ or a factual assessment of Chinese or American people.

## Supply-line interpretation

The model can represent positive, neutral, or negative conditions across supply
lines such as energy, agricultural inputs, semiconductors, machinery, shipping,
and strategic materials. It is an analytical abstraction rather than a moral
judgment about a country.

## Sources

- China Customs: 2025 total import/export statistics.
- U.S. trade reporting: 2025 U.S.-China goods trade.
- July 2026 customs reporting for the import-growth observation.
