# China — 2006 Economy

## National accounts
- Preliminary GDP: **209,407 hundred million yuan**.
- Primary industry: **24,700 hundred million yuan**.
- Secondary industry: **102,004 hundred million yuan**.
- Tertiary industry: **82,703 hundred million yuan**.
- Preliminary real GDP growth: **10.7%**.

## Later verified GDP note
NBS later published final verified 2006 GDP of **211,923 hundred million yuan**, with real growth **11.6%**; primary **24,040**, secondary **103,162**, tertiary **84,721** hundred million yuan. The archive retains the contemporaneous communiqué figures above and records the later revision here.

Sources: NBS 2006 Statistical Communiqué; NBS 2008 final GDP verification.
