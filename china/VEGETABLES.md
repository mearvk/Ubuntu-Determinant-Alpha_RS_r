# China Vegetables

## 2025 output

NBS reported that China's production of **vegetables, including edible fungi, reached about 890 million tonnes in 2025**. This was **18.6% higher than in 2020**.

## Production system

Vegetable production covers leafy vegetables, brassicas, roots and tubers, alliums, solanaceous crops, legumes, cucurbits, and other horticultural crops. Edible fungi are included in the NBS aggregate cited above.

## Geographic pattern

Production is distributed across China's major agricultural regions. Climate, irrigation, transport access, urban demand, protected cultivation, and regional specialization all affect the location and timing of production.

## Economic role

Vegetables are important not only as food but as a high-frequency agricultural commodity supporting farm income, wholesale markets, cold-chain logistics, processing, retail, food service, and interregional trade.

## Data note

The 890-million-tonne figure is an official NBS aggregate for vegetables including edible fungi. It should not be compared directly with datasets that define vegetables differently.

## Source

National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2026 report on agricultural and rural development during the 14th Five-Year Plan.
