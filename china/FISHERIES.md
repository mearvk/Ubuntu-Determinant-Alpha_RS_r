# China Fisheries and Aquaculture

## 2025 production

China produced **76.57 million tonnes of aquatic products** in 2025, up **4.1%** from 2024.

| Segment | Output | Growth |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cultured aquatic products | 63.24 Mt | +4.4% |
| Capture fisheries | 13.33 Mt | +2.7% |
| Total | 76.57 Mt | +4.1% |

Aquaculture therefore represented roughly 83% of the official total in 2025.

## Production systems

The sector includes freshwater pond and lake aquaculture, reservoir production, coastal and marine aquaculture, marine capture fisheries, freshwater capture fisheries, hatcheries, feed, processing, cold-chain logistics, wholesale markets, and retail.

## Economic significance

Aquaculture allows substantial food production from water surfaces and controlled production systems. Capture fisheries remain important but are subject to resource-management, environmental, weather, and stock constraints.

## Source

National Bureau of Statistics of China, *Statistical Communiqué of the PRC on the 2025 National Economic and Social Development*, February 2026.
