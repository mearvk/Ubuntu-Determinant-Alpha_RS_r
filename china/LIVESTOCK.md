# China Livestock

## 2025 baseline

China's livestock system includes swine, cattle, sheep, poultry, dairy cattle, laying hens, broilers, and associated breeding and feed industries.

Key 2025 indicators reported by NBS:

- Pork: **59.38 Mt**.
- Beef: **8.01 Mt**.
- Mutton: **4.96 Mt**.
- Poultry meat: **28.37 Mt**.
- Milk: **40.91 Mt**.
- Eggs: **34.98 Mt**.
- Year-end pig inventory: **429.67 million head**.
- Pigs slaughtered: **719.73 million head**.

## Feed connection

Livestock creates a major downstream demand for feed grains and oilseed meals. Corn is a particularly important feed crop, while soybean products are important sources of protein in feed formulations.

## Regional diversity

Livestock production ranges from intensive commercial farms near major feed and consumer markets to pastoral and mixed farming systems in western and northern regions.

## Economic chain

Breeding -> feed -> animal production -> veterinary services -> slaughter -> processing -> cold chain -> wholesale -> retail/food service.

## Source

National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2025 national economic and social development statistics.
