# China 2019 — Farming Statistics

## Status
AVAILABLE

## Key observations
- Grain sown area: **116.06 million ha**.
- Wheat: **23.73 million ha**; rice: **29.69 million ha**; corn: **41.28 million ha**.
- Cotton: **3.34 million ha**; oil-bearing: **12.93 million ha**; sugar: **1.62 million ha**.
- New effective irrigation: **over 0.27 million ha**.
- New water-saving irrigation: **1.46 million ha**.

## Source
National Bureau of Statistics, 2019 Statistical Communiqué; irrigation data sourced from the Ministry of Water Resources.
