# China 2022 Statistical Archive

Annual statistical container for China, using the archive's CASR methodology.

Categories: economy, agriculture, farming statistics, produce, grains, meat, livestock, vegetables, fruit, and water.

Primary source: National Bureau of Statistics of China. Supplementary official agencies are used where identified.

Status: historical annual record. Figures are preserved from the cited official annual releases; `NOT_AVAILABLE` is used where the reviewed source does not provide an exact observation.
