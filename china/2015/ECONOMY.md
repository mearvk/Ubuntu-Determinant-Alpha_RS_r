# China 2015 — Economy

- GDP: 67,670.8 billion yuan; growth 6.9%.
- Primary industry: 6,086.3 billion yuan; growth 3.9%.
- Secondary industry: 27,427.8 billion yuan; growth 6.0%.
- Tertiary industry: 34,156.7 billion yuan; growth 8.3%.
- GDP shares: primary 9.0%; secondary 40.5%; tertiary 50.5%.

Note: contemporaneous communiqué figures are preliminary.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2015 Statistical Communiqué.
