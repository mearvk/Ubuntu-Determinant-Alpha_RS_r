# China 2015 — Farming Statistics

- Grain sown area: 113.34 million ha.
- Cotton sown area: 3.80 million ha.
- Oil-bearing crops sown area: 14.06 million ha.
- Sugar crops sown area: 1.74 million ha.
- New effective irrigation area: over 1.58 million ha.
- New water-saving irrigation area: over 2.54 million ha.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2015 Statistical Communiqué.
