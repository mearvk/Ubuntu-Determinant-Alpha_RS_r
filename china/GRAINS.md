# China Grain Production

## 2025 national totals

| Crop/group | Sown area | Output | Yield |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| All grain | 119.409 million ha | 714.876 Mt | 5,986.8 kg/ha |
| Cereals | 100.619 million ha | 660.207 Mt | 6,561.5 kg/ha |
| Rice | 29.012 million ha | 209.041 Mt | 7,205.3 kg/ha |
| Wheat | 23.582 million ha | 140.072 Mt | 5,939.8 kg/ha |
| Corn | 44.961 million ha | 301.235 Mt | 6,700.0 kg/ha |
| Beans | 11.780 million ha | 23.932 Mt | 2,031.5 kg/ha |
| Tubers | 7.010 million ha | 30.737 Mt | 4,384.9 kg/ha |

## Annual result

Total grain output rose **1.2%** in 2025. Rice output rose 0.7%, wheat was approximately unchanged, corn rose 2.1%, and soybean output rose 1.3%.

## Seasonal structure

- Summer grain: **149.746 Mt**.
- Early rice: **28.513 Mt**.
- Autumn grain: **536.617 Mt**.

## Food-system significance

Grain production supplies direct human consumption, animal feed, industrial processing, seed systems, and strategic food reserves. Corn is particularly important to feed and industrial demand; rice and wheat are major direct food staples.

## Source

National Bureau of Statistics of China, *Announcement on 2025 Grain Output Data*, 12 December 2025.
