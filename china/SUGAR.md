# China Sugar Crops

## 2025 baseline

NBS reported **117.01 million tonnes** of sugar-crop output in 2025, up **2.1%** from 2024. Sown area was approximately **1.49 million hectares**.

## Main crops

China's sugar economy includes sugarcane and sugar beet, with production geography influenced strongly by climate, water, soil, temperature, and processing access.

## Processing chain

Farm production feeds mills and processors, which convert cane or beet into sugar and co-products. Transport distance between farms and processing facilities matters because harvested cane and beet are bulky and time-sensitive.

## Source

National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2025 national economic and social development statistics.
