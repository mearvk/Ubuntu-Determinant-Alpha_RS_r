# Water — Annual Statistical Record

The parent directory identifies the year. Water covers agricultural water resources, irrigation and related infrastructure relevant to the food-production system.

## Indicators
- Effective/irrigated agricultural area.
- Water withdrawals and use where published.
- Reservoirs and water-conservancy infrastructure.
- Flood-control, drainage and soil/water conservation indicators.
- Rural water and agricultural-water-management indicators where comparable.
- Water-resource indicators from AQUASTAT when internationally standardized data are required.

## Method
NBS agriculture publications identify irrigation, reservoirs and water-conservancy statistics and note their relationship to Ministry of Water Resources reporting. Keep physical water quantities separate from infrastructure counts and irrigated-area measures.
