# Water — Annual Statistical Record

The parent directory identifies the year. Water covers agricultural water resources, irrigation and related infrastructure relevant to the food-production system.

> **Metadata:** `record_type=annual_water` · `year=1965` · `geography=China` · `scope=national` · `status=historical_actual` · `units=source_specific` · `price_basis=commodity_price` · `methodology=historical_source_preservation`

## Methods

The purpose of this record is to preserve water and irrigation evidence as part of the conditions that made agricultural production possible. It is intended for **college and university study, professional resource and agricultural analysis, and long-term historical research**. The WHY is to keep infrastructure, water quantities, irrigation, and prices conceptually separate so future readers can understand both what was measured and what cannot responsibly be inferred from it.

Water evidence can also become relevant to **legal, congressional, institutional, and moral inquiry** involving public resources, infrastructure, food security, environmental decisions, and historical responsibility. The method is therefore to preserve provenance and uncertainty rather than manufacture a tariff or value from unrelated statistics. This is an evidentiary framework for responsible historical analysis, not legal advice or political advocacy.

## Method
Water-resource quantities, irrigation infrastructure and commodity-price observations are distinct statistical measures. The archive does not infer a water price from agricultural output or later-period tariffs.
