# Grains — Annual Statistical Record

The parent directory identifies the year. Grain statistics cover the major cereal, bean and tuber components used in China's national grain-production accounting.

## Core series
- Grain sown area.
- Grain output.
- Grain yield per hectare.
- Rice.
- Wheat.
- Corn/maize.
- Soybeans and other beans.
- Sorghum, millet and miscellaneous grains.
- Tubers where included by the source definition.

## Definition control
Historical NBS definitions and conversion rules can change. For example, historical grain accounting has included rice, wheat, corn, sorghum, millet, miscellaneous grains, beans and tubers under specified conversion rules. Never silently splice incompatible definitions.
