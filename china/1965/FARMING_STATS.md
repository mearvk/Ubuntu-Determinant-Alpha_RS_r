# Farming Statistics — Annual Statistical Record

The parent directory identifies the year. This document provides the farming-statistics framework used to interpret annual agricultural performance.

## Indicators
- Farm and rural labor measures.
- Sown area and harvested area.
- Yield per hectare.
- Agricultural machinery ownership and mechanization.
- Irrigated area and agricultural water use where available.
- Fertilizer application.
- Disaster-affected and disaster-stricken area where reported.
- Agricultural production value and indices.
