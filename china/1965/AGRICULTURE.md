# Agriculture — Annual Statistical Record

The parent directory identifies the year. This document covers national agricultural production and the farming system for that year.

## Indicators
- Cultivated/sown area and major crop areas.
- Total agricultural, forestry, animal-husbandry and fishery output value.
- Grain production and yield.
- Oil crops, cotton, sugar crops and other major field crops.
- Agricultural machinery and mechanization where published.
- Fertilizer use and irrigation indicators where published.
- Rural labor and agricultural structure where comparable.

## Statistical discipline
Definitions and survey systems changed over time. Historical comparisons must retain the source's units, coverage and methodological notes. NBS agriculture tables cover farming, forestry, animal husbandry and fishery activities and document their collection methods.

## Sources
Primary: NBS China Statistical Yearbook. Supplement with competent sector agencies and FAOSTAT where standardized cross-year series are required.
