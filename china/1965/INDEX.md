# China Annual Statistical Archive

This year directory is the archival container for the requested China statistics. The category documents at `../` define the scope and source methodology for Economy, Agriculture, Produce, Farming Statistics, Grains, Meat, Livestock, Vegetables, Fruit, Water, Fisheries, Forestry, Oil Crops, Dairy & Eggs, Tea, Cotton and Sugar.

## Methods

The purpose of this archive is not merely to collect numbers, but to preserve a durable evidence base for why those numbers matter. It is designed to serve **college and university study**, **professional research**, and long-term historical analysis by keeping each observation tied to its source, definition, year, and methodological context. That makes the record useful for students learning how evidence is constructed, professionals comparing economic and agricultural systems, and researchers who need to distinguish an observed historical fact from a later reconstruction, estimate, or interpretation.

The longer-term purpose is also **legal, congressional, institutional, and moral**: to provide a traceable historical record that can support serious public-policy analysis, legislative research, oversight, historical inquiry, and ethical judgment without turning the archive itself into advocacy. A durable record should allow future readers to ask not only *what happened*, but *what evidence supports the claim, what was known at the time, what definitions were used, and why a later conclusion should or should not be trusted*. The method therefore favors provenance, transparency, comparability controls, and explicit uncertainty over convenient numbers or unsupported conclusions.

## Status

The annual directory structure is established for 1965–2026. A category is `AVAILABLE` only when an authoritative source publishes an observation for the specific year and series. Otherwise the record must remain `NOT_AVAILABLE`.

## Source hierarchy

1. China National Bureau of Statistics for directly comparable national annual statistics.
2. Other competent Chinese statistical ministries/agencies for sector-specific series.
3. FAOSTAT for internationally standardized agricultural, livestock, fisheries and forestry time series.
4. FAO AQUASTAT for water resources, withdrawals, irrigation and agricultural-water-management series.

## 2026

2026 is an incomplete live year. Partial releases must be identified as such; a final annual total must not be manufactured from partial data.

## Historical note

The archive begins in 1965 because that is the requested boundary, while FAOSTAT and AQUASTAT contain many series beginning earlier. Individual commodities and indicators have different start years and revisions.

## Sources

- National Bureau of Statistics of China (NBS), *China Statistical Yearbook* and historical annual statistical database — principal national statistical source and methodology reference.
- Other competent Chinese statistical ministries and agencies — sector-specific observations where NBS is not the originating agency.
- FAOSTAT — internationally standardized agricultural, livestock, fisheries and forestry series.
- FAO AQUASTAT — water resources, withdrawals, irrigation and agricultural-water-management series.
- China Statistical Press / China Statistics Press publications — historical statistical yearbooks and official statistical compilations.
