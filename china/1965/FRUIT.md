# Fruit — Annual Statistical Record

The parent directory identifies the year. Fruit covers orchard, tree-fruit and related horticultural production included in official agricultural statistics.

## Indicators
- Orchard/fruit-bearing area where published.
- Fruit output.
- Major fruit categories where comparable.
- Yield indicators where available.
- Melons and related horticultural produce when separately reported.

## Interpretation
Fruit production is geographically and climatically diverse. Annual records should retain the source's commodity definitions and distinguish production from planted area. Historical series should be updated when later NBS yearbooks explicitly revise them.

Reference: https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/ndsj/
