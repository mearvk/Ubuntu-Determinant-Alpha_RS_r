# Meat — Annual Statistical Record

The parent directory identifies the year. Meat covers national slaughter-output series and major species used in official animal-product statistics.

## Core series
- Pork.
- Beef.
- Mutton/goat meat.
- Poultry meat where published.
- Total meat output where the source definition permits.
- Per-species slaughter and production indicators where available.

## Methodological control
NBS explains that animal-product statistics have undergone census and survey adjustments. Since 1999, major animal-husbandry products have used sample-survey data for finalized national statistics. Earlier and later series therefore require attention to source notes before direct comparison.

Reference: https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/Statisticaldata/yearbook/
