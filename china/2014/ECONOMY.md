# China 2014 — Economy

- GDP: 63,646.3 billion yuan; growth 7.4%.
- Primary industry: 5,833.2 billion yuan; growth 4.1%.
- Secondary industry: 27,139.2 billion yuan; growth 7.3%.
- Tertiary industry: 30,673.9 billion yuan; growth 8.1%.
- GDP shares: primary 9.2%; secondary 42.6%; tertiary 48.2%.

Note: contemporaneous communiqué figures are preliminary.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2014 Statistical Communiqué.
