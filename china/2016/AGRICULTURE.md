# China 2016 — Agriculture

- Grain sown area: 113.03 million ha.
- Cotton: 3.38 million ha sown; 5.34 million tons output.
- Oil-bearing crops: 14.12 million ha; 36.13 million tons.
- Sugar crops: 1.68 million ha; 122.99 million tons.
- Grain output: 616.24 million tons.
- Tea: 2.41 million tons.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2016 Statistical Communiqué.
