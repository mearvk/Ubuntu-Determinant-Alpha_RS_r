# China 2016 — Farming Statistics

- Grain sown area: 113.03 million ha.
- Wheat: 24.19 million ha; rice: 30.16 million ha; corn: 36.76 million ha.
- Cotton: 3.38 million ha; oil-bearing crops: 14.12 million ha; sugar crops: 1.68 million ha.
- New effective irrigation: over 1.18 million ha.
- New water-saving irrigation: over 2.11 million ha.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2016 Statistical Communiqué.
