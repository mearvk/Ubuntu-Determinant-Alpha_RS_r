# China Tea Production

## 2025 output

NBS reported **3.92 million tonnes** of tea production in 2025, up **4.8%** from 2024 and **33.6% above 2020** in the Five-Year-Plan comparison.

## Economic characteristics

Tea is a high-value agricultural commodity with extensive regional specialization. The economic chain includes plantations, processing, grading, packaging, domestic wholesale, retail, hospitality, and international trade.

## Production geography

Tea cultivation is concentrated mainly in suitable humid and subtropical environments, with important production areas in southern and southeastern China. Elevation, rainfall, temperature, soil, cultivar, and processing traditions affect product type and quality.

## Source

National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2025 national statistics and 2026 agricultural achievement report.
