# China — 2009 Produce

- Cotton: 640万吨; later revised: 638万吨
- Oil-bearing crops: 3,100万吨; later revised: 3,154万吨
- Sugar crops: 12,200万吨; later revised: 12,277万吨
- Tobacco: 280万吨
- Tea: 135万吨
- Vegetables + edible fungi: 61,824万吨 (later NBS revised series)
- Fruit: 12,246万吨 (garden fruit; excludes fruit-use melons; later revised series)

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China.
