# China Meat Production

## 2025 baseline

NBS reported **100.72 million tonnes** of pork, beef, mutton and poultry meat in 2025, up **4.2%** year over year.

| Category | 2025 output | Change from 2024 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Pork | 59.38 Mt | +4.1% |
| Beef | 8.01 Mt | +2.8% |
| Mutton | 4.96 Mt | -4.2% |
| Poultry meat | 28.37 Mt | +6.7% |

NBS's broader meat total, which includes additional meat categories, was **101.92 million tonnes** in its Five-Year-Plan agricultural review.

## Livestock indicators

- Pigs at year-end: **429.67 million head**.
- Pigs slaughtered during 2025: **719.73 million head**.
- Milk output: **40.91 million tonnes**.
- Egg output: **34.98 million tonnes**.

## Production interpretation

Pork remains the dominant meat category by tonnage. Poultry is the second-largest category in the NBS four-category series and recorded the fastest growth among those four categories in 2025. Beef and mutton represent smaller but economically important ruminant sectors.

## Supply-chain context

The meat economy includes breeding, feed production, veterinary services, slaughter, processing, refrigeration, logistics, wholesale markets, retail, restaurants, and household consumption. Production statistics should therefore be read as physical output rather than as total economic value.
