# China 1966 — FRUIT

- **Year:** 1966
- **Geography:** China, national aggregate
- **Method:** Historical category record; no interpolation or back-casting.

## Annual observations

A verified 1966 national total for fruit production, together with a complete species breakdown, has not yet been entered.

## Data policy

Fruit totals and species series are kept separate from general produce statistics because historical definitions and coverage can change. Unverified values remain N/A rather than being estimated.

Primary reference: National Bureau of Statistics of China, Statistical Yearbooks: https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/Statisticaldata/yearbook/
