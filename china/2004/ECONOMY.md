# China — 2004 Economy

## National accounts
- GDP: **136,515 hundred million yuan**.
- Primary industry: **20,744 hundred million yuan**.
- Secondary industry: **72,387 hundred million yuan**.
- Tertiary industry: **43,384 hundred million yuan**.
- Real GDP growth: **9.5%**.
