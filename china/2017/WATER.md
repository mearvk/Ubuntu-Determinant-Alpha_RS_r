# China 2017 — Water

- Aquatic products: 69.38 million tons; cultured 52.81 million; fished 16.56 million.
- New effective irrigation: over 1.09 million ha.
- New water-saving irrigation: over 1.44 million ha.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2017 Statistical Communiqué; water/sector data attributed to competent Chinese agencies.
