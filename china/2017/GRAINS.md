# China 2017 — Grains

- Total grain: 617.91 million tons.
- Summer grain: 140.31 million tons.
- Early rice: 31.74 million tons.
- Autumn grain: 445.85 million tons.
- Rice: 208.56 million tons; wheat: 129.77 million tons; corn: 215.89 million tons.

Source: National Bureau of Statistics of China, 2017 Statistical Communiqué.
