# Korea — Prices Annual Record

Archive year: containing directory. Covers consumer prices, producer prices, export/import prices, and related price indices.

## Standard indicators
- Consumer Price Index (CPI)
- Producer Price Index (PPI)
- Export and import price indices
- Inflation rates and price-level changes

## Method
Use KOSIS and Bank of Korea official price statistics. Preserve index base years, basket revisions, weights, and coverage. Never treat an index as a currency price without its definition. `N/A` is not zero. 2026 may be partial.

## Sources
KOSIS, Ministry of Data and Statistics, Bank of Korea, and responsible official agencies.
