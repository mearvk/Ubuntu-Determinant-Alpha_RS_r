## Method
Capture fisheries and aquaculture are distinct production sources. National domestic totals may also include offshore and inland components and therefore should not be substituted mechanically for an FAO capture-plus-aquaculture series. Source definitions, revisions, and preliminary status are preserved rather than silently reconciled.

## Sources
KOSIS fishery production tables; Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries statistical portal and annual releases; FAO Fishery and Aquaculture Statistics / FishStatJ. KOSIS currently publishes monthly fishery production tables, while the Ministry's 2025 release reports 3.93 million tonnes total and 2.53 million tonnes of marine aquaculture.
