## Methods — WHY

Meat production, slaughter, inventory, consumption and retail prices are different statistical concepts and are not substituted for one another. Historical series can change definition or coverage; `N/A` is retained rather than estimated or back-cast.

## Sources

- KOSIS national statistical database and historical meat-production tables.
- MAFRA/KASS agricultural and livestock statistics.
- KAMIS historical livestock retail-price data where an exact item, grade and unit are available.
