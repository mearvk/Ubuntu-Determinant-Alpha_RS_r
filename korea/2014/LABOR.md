# Korea — Labor Annual Record

Archive year: containing directory. Covers labor force, employment, unemployment, participation, occupations, and hours where comparable annual observations are available.

## Standard indicators
- Economically active population
- Employed and unemployed persons
- Labor-force participation rate
- Employment-to-population ratio
- Unemployment rate
- Hours and occupational structure

`N/A` means not verified for the containing year; it is not zero.

## Method
Use KOSIS labor-force and establishment statistics, preserving survey design, population definitions, and classification revisions. 2026 observations can be partial-year.

## Sources
KOSIS, Ministry of Data and Statistics, and responsible official labor-statistics agencies.
