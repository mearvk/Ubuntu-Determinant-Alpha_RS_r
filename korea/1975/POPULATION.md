# Korea — Population Annual Record

Archive year: containing directory. This domain records population, households, vital events, migration, and demographic structure. `N/A` means a compatible observation was not independently verified for the containing year; it is not zero.

## Standard indicators
- Mid-year / resident population
- Population density
- Live births and deaths
- Total fertility rate
- Life expectancy
- Households and household size
- Internal and international migration

## Method
Use KOSIS population census, resident-registration, vital-statistics, and population-estimate series. Preserve original definitions, units, geography, and reference periods. Do not silently back-cast later definitions into earlier years. 2026 is treated as an incomplete/current statistical year where applicable.

## Sources
KOSIS (Korean Statistical Information Service), Ministry of Data and Statistics, and relevant official demographic tables. KOSIS identifies Population as a dedicated statistical subject and provides annual and historical statistical tables.
