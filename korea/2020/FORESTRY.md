## Method
Forest area, standing stock, production value, reforestation, forest management, and timber supply are separate measures. Historical survey redesigns and revisions are not silently back-cast. The national forest-resource survey began with UNDP/FAO support in 1964 and later moved to a recurring inventory design.

## Sources
Korea Forest Service Statistical Yearbook / National Forest Inventory; Statistics Korea (KOSIS); MAFRA Statistical Yearbook; FAO Global Forest Resources Assessment for internationally harmonized forest definitions. The KFS series reports 6.298 million ha and 165.2 m³/ha in 2020 and 6.291 million ha and 172.4 m³/ha in 2022.
