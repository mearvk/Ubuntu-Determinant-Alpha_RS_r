# Korea Archive Metadata

## Scope

- Geography: Republic of Korea / South Korea
- Archive period: 1965-2026
- Archive class: historical economic, agricultural, food, livestock, produce, and water documentation
- Compiler: MEARVK LLC / Max Rupplin — archive compiler
- Intended audiences: college/academic research, professional analysis, government/public-institution research, congressional/public-policy work, legal/historical research, institutional archives, and general reference

## Source posture

The archive is source-aware and evidence-led. Primary Korean statistical institutions are preferred. KOSIS provides a central statistical database covering agriculture and forestry, economy and market, prices, national accounts, and related domains. KAMIS provides agricultural/livestock/fishery market-price series, including annual and historical views. MAFRA publishes agricultural and food statistical yearbooks. The Bank of Korea publishes national accounts and methodological documentation.

## Science Grade

6+ — documentation-quality grade for structured archival work. It is not a claim of experimental validity, causal identification, or statistical significance.

## Methods — WHY

The annual documents exist so that later readers can distinguish primary observations, historical definitions, derived measures, and unresolved gaps. This supports reproducible academic work, professional review, institutional continuity, and long-term legal or public-policy reference without silently converting assumptions into facts.

The archive also preserves negative knowledge: when a requested historical series has not yet been verified, the document records that status instead of manufacturing continuity. That makes later source acquisition and revision auditable.

## Source discipline

1. Prefer original government/statistical publications.
2. Preserve the source year and publication/revision date.
3. Preserve units and definitions exactly before deriving alternate measures.
4. Mark missing, incompatible, reconstructed, or estimated observations explicitly.
5. Do not backfill historical years from modern summaries unless the summary explicitly documents the historical series.
6. Derived model outputs must remain distinguishable from source observations.

## Annual document rule

For each `YYYY/` directory, `YYYY/META.md` controls metadata and `YYYY/INDEX.md` indexes the twelve domain documents. Every domain document must refer back to `./META.md` and identify its source status.
