# Greenland Agriculture — 2025

Greenland agriculture is primarily based on sheep farming with summer grazing. Winter feed, potatoes, and vegetables are produced for local use; agriculture is concentrated mainly in the deep fjords of South Greenland because of the sub-Arctic climate.

## Data status
The latest published *Greenland in Figures* material should be treated as the reference for current livestock and farm counts. Annual values are not copied here unless verified at table level.
