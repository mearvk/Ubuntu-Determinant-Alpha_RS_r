# Greenland Agriculture — 2026

Greenland agriculture is primarily based on sheep farming with summer grazing. Winter feed, potatoes, and vegetables are produced for local use; agriculture is concentrated mainly in the deep fjords of South Greenland because of the sub-Arctic climate.

## Data status
2026 is the current archive year. Current-year agricultural observations must be treated as preliminary or unavailable until the relevant Statistics Greenland tables are published and verified. No unverified 2026 values are asserted here.
