# Greenland Agriculture — 2024

Greenland agriculture remained concentrated in South Greenland. Sheep farming and summer grazing continued as the principal agricultural activities, with winter feed, potatoes, and vegetables supporting local production.

## Verified reference data
Statistics Greenland reports 15,750 sheep, 1,190 tame reindeer, 90 cows, 115 horses, 215 fowls, and no reported beehive count in its published 2024 agriculture series.

## Methods — WHY
The published livestock figures are retained as reference observations. Crop series and additional agricultural measures require table-level verification before inclusion.
