# Greenland Agriculture — 1965

## Scope

This annual agriculture record covers Greenland in 1965. Agriculture was geographically concentrated in the more favorable South Greenland fjord districts and was constrained by the Arctic/sub-Arctic climate, limited arable land, and the importance of pastoral production.

## Historical record

Greenland agriculture is principally associated with sheep farming and summer grazing, with winter feed production supporting livestock. Potatoes and vegetables were also grown for local use. Agriculture should not be interpreted as a major nationwide land-use sector merely because statistical definitions of agricultural land include pasture.

## Data status

- Agricultural land: **2,350 km²** in the FAO-derived historical series.
- Crop production: annual crop-level observations require table-level source verification.
- Livestock: annual counts require table-level source verification.
- Geographic concentration: South Greenland.

## Methods — WHY

The archive separates verified observations from later summaries and reconstructions. Values are not estimated where an identified historical source is absent. Definitions, units, geographic coverage, and publication/revision status should be preserved when later observations are added.
