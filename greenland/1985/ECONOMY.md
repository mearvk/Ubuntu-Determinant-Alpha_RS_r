# Greenland Economy — {{YEAR}}

## Scope

This annual record documents the economy of Greenland for {{YEAR}}. Greenland's economy is shaped by fisheries, public administration, transport, construction, trade, tourism, and other service activity, with the scale and composition of these sectors changing over time.

## Economic record

- **National accounts:** Statistics Greenland is the principal source for GDP, gross value added, income accounts, and related measures.
- **Fisheries and exports:** Fisheries and fish products are a major component of Greenland's external trade and economic activity.
- **Public sector:** Public administration and government-supported services form an important part of economic activity.
- **Trade:** Imports and exports should be recorded from Statistics Greenland's foreign-trade tables using their published definitions and units.
- **Labour and income:** Employment, unemployment, earnings, and household-income measures should be taken from the corresponding Statistics Greenland tables when available.

## Data status

{{STATUS}}

Historical annual figures are not inferred where a directly identified source series has not been verified. Where modern national-account series begin later than the requested archive period, earlier years remain explicitly identified as requiring historical-source verification.

## Methods — WHY

Preserve original definitions, units, geographic scope, valuation basis, price basis, publication date, and revision status. Current-price and chained-volume measures must not be substituted for one another. Do not compare nominal values across decades without accounting for the applicable price basis and currency conventions.

## Primary source framework

- Statistics Greenland / Statbank Greenland — national accounts, foreign trade, labour market, income, public finances, prices, businesses, fisheries, and tourism.
- Historical Danish statistical publications and Greenland administration records where Statistics Greenland's modern series do not extend to the requested year.
- World Bank and other international datasets may provide comparable series for later periods, but should remain secondary to the underlying Greenland statistical source.
