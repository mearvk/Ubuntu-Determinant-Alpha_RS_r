# Greenland Agriculture — 2016

Greenland agriculture remained concentrated in South Greenland, with sheep farming and summer grazing central to the sector. Winter feed, potatoes, and vegetables supported local production.

## Verified reference data
Statistics Greenland reports 18,190 sheep, 3,000 tame reindeer, 155 cows, 137 horses, 211 fowls, and 6 beehives for 2016 in its agriculture series.

## Methods — WHY
The livestock figures above are retained as published reference observations. Crop series and additional agricultural measures require table-level verification before inclusion.
