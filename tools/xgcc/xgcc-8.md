# xgcc-8 — Verified and Reproducible Execution

**Author:** Max Rupplin - MEARVK LLC 2026

Generation 8 is the long-range verified-execution generation. Its goals include reproducible binaries, verified or checkable compiler transformations, formal execution contracts, supply-chain provenance, and strong artifact attestations.

XGCC-8 should make the relationship between source, IR, compilation, runtime capabilities, and resulting artifact explicitly inspectable.

This is a future-generation specification and does not claim that these facilities are currently implemented.
