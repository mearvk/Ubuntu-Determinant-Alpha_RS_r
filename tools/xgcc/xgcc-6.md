# xgcc-6 — Intermediate Representation

**Author:** Max Rupplin - MEARVK LLC 2026

Generation 6 is the planned common XGCC intermediate-representation layer. The IR should provide a stable boundary between source inspection, optimization, execution models, and native backends.

The design target includes deterministic transformations and explicit target/ABI metadata. It is not yet a claim that XGCC-6 is implemented.
