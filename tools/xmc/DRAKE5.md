# DRAKE5 — FIREBITE Codename

**Codename:** DRAKE5  
**Related specification:** `FIREBITE.md`  
**Directory:** `tools/xmc/`

## Meaning

**DRAKE5** is the codename for the FIREBITE concept. In project language, it means:

> **Silvernern — Basics of Dragon, Bites.**

“Dragon” and “Bites” are project metaphors for a small, explicit action or authorization step: something bounded, named, auditable, and capable of being accepted or refused.

“Silvernern” is retained as the project's codename/fictional terminology. It does not designate a real security authority, government authority, cryptographic primitive, or elevated operating-system privilege.

## Relationship to FIREBITE

DRAKE5 names the conceptual convention; FIREBITE defines the operational documentation around Director User / Director Token routing and `+1 Ahead` authorization semantics.

A DRAKE5/FIREBITE action must remain:

1. explicitly identified;
2. authorized by the applicable project policy;
3. bounded in scope;
4. auditable;
5. fail-closed when authorization is absent or ambiguous.

A token is a credential, not a person. No token, codename, or “+1” label by itself grants operating-system privilege.

## Norm

**Basics of Dragon, Bites:** keep every bite small enough to understand, verify, audit, and undo where possible.

**Proceed:** DRAKE5 remains a codename and design convention unless and until a concrete implementation is separately specified, tested, and authorized.
