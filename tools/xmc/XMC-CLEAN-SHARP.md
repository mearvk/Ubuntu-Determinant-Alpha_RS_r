# XMC: Clean, Sharp, Cleans

> **XMC is clean, but it is sharp; cleans.**

This is the concise engineering principle for XMC.

- **Clean** — keep interfaces, source, metadata, and generated output understandable and disciplined.
- **Sharp** — validate inputs, reject ambiguity, enforce explicit boundaries, and fail safely.
- **Cleans** — remove unsafe, malformed, stale, or ambiguous states instead of silently carrying them forward.
- **Controlled** — no hidden privilege, shell interpretation, or implied authority.
- **Auditable** — important actions and decisions should have an identifiable reason and boundary.

The phrase is a design principle, not a security claim. XMC functionality remains subject to its actual implementation, tests, and documented authorization model.
