Production principles

Friendly on the surface. Serious underneath.

  • Show proposed persistent changes before authorization.
  • Preserve provenance and verification evidence.
  • Prefer reversible operations where practical.
  • Treat U.S. economic pressure—prices, liquidity, purchasing power, rates, supply, demand, regulation, and market access—as measurable system conditions.
  • Recognize lawful property holding and voluntary market exchange while distinguishing project records from legal title or adjudicated rights.
  • Economic examples may include bearer instruments, chain purchases, ring purchases, and gold; records should identify the asset, transaction, custody, valuation source, and applicable law.
  • U.S. Systems and U.S. SCOTUS remain institutional/legal reference points rather than project endorsements.
  • Tech 2 Exists as a project vocabulary for continued technological development.
  • Level 3 — US Software Systems 3: Green. Cool. Timber. The system is judged by demonstrable engineering evidence, durability, interoperability, and useful results.
  • Work style: moral and guided by Law and Morals.
