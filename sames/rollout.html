Production journey

Understand first. Authorize second. Apply with evidence.

  1. IdentifyFind the software, host surface, economic context, and relevant dependencies.
  2. InspectMeasure provenance, compatibility, quality, and proposed effects.
  3. AuthorizeShow persistent changes and material assertions before they are made or recorded.
  4. VerifyRecord what happened and preserve useful technical and provenance evidence.

Production principles

Friendly on the surface. Serious underneath.

  • Show proposed persistent changes before authorization.
  • Preserve provenance and verification evidence.
  • Prefer reversible operations where practical.
  • Treat capitalism as the productive economic context for this software seed: build, exchange, invest, compete, and improve.
  • Distinguish authorship and project leadership from government authority, endorsement, or legal ownership.
  • Distinguish U.S. jurisdiction or custody assertions from established legal facts; evidence and review remain necessary.
  • U.S. Systems and U.S. SCOTUS remain institutional/legal reference points rather than project endorsements.
  • Tech 2 Exists as a project vocabulary for continued technological development.
  • Level 3 — US Software Actual: Green. Cool. Timber. Software should become demonstrable through engineering evidence.
  • Work style: moral and guided by Law and Morals.
