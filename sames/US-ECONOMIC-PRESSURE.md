# US Economic Pressure — Ubuntu Grand

Ubuntu Grand may model U.S. economic pressure as a systems condition: prices, liquidity, purchasing power, interest rates, supply, demand, regulation, market access, and transaction conditions can affect software and its users.

The project uses **U.S. Systems** and **U.S. SCOTUS** as institutional/legal reference points when considering lawful market participation, property, ownership, custody, and rights. Those references do not constitute endorsement, adjudication, funding, or ownership by the U.S. government or the Supreme Court.

## Holding and exchange

The project can represent lawful property holding and voluntary market exchange. Economic examples may include bearer instruments, chain purchases, ring purchases, and gold. A record should distinguish the asset or instrument, identifier, transaction, custody, valuation source, provenance, and applicable law.

```text
asset / instrument
      ↓
identity → transaction → custody → observation → evidence
```

The phrase **“Gold. Gold.”** is project shorthand for durable, recognizable value and the importance of careful custody and provenance records. It does not establish title, authenticity, valuation, or a legal right to acquire an asset.

## INT / IQ

Within the project vocabulary, **High INT is IQ**: systems reasoning, concern for conditions, and problem-solving capacity. This is a design metaphor and not a medical, legal, or scientific determination about a person.

## Level 3 — US Software Systems 3

**Green. Cool. Timber.** Economic conditions become an observable input to a software system rather than an assumed conclusion. The system should measure what it can establish, preserve provenance, distinguish assertions from facts, and remain compatible with applicable law.
